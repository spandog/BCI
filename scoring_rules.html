SCORING RULES

How the Book Club Invitational is won and lost

1Win
½Tie
0Loss
Day 1
Pairs Matchplay
Quinta do Vale Golf Course

  • Each team fields six pairs, with each pair facing one pair from the opposing team — producing six matches in total.

  • Each pair plays matchplay — holes are won, lost or halved rather than counting total strokes.

  • The best nett score on each hole wins that hole for the pair. Handicap strokes are applied per the stroke index.

  • The pair who wins the most holes wins the match. A match that is level after 18 holes is halved.

  • A match may be conceded early once one pair cannot mathematically be caught.

Day 2
Individual Matchplay
Isla Canela Links Course

  • Each player competes in their own singles matchplay match against an opponent from the opposing team.

  • The best nett score on each hole wins that hole. Handicap strokes are applied per the stroke index.

  • The player who wins the most holes wins the match. A match level after 18 holes is halved.

  • With 12 singles matches, there are 12 points available on Day 2.

Overall Result

  • 18 points are available across the two days — 6 on Day 1, 12 on Day 2.
  • The team with the most points at the end of Day 2 wins the Book Club Invitational.
  • 9.5 points are needed to win outright. A tie at 9–9 retains the trophy for the defending champions.
